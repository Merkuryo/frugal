# Decide: choose between options, design, plan

1. **Frame.** The decision, the constraints, what "good" means (criteria with rough weights), and how reversible it is.
2. **Options.** 2–4 genuinely different options, including "do nothing" or "smallest change" when it applies.
3. **Facts before opinions.** Anything an experiment, prototype or lookup can settle: settle it, cheaply.
4. **Think.** Send `thinker` the frame, the options and the facts. Ask for a ranked recommendation with reasons and the main risk of each.
5. **Panel only at L2.** If the decision is costly to reverse and `thinker` is not clearly confident, add one independent second opinion from a different model (e.g. `worker` on sonnet) and compare. Two opinions maximum.
6. **Decide or hand over.** Taste, values or irreversible commitments go to the human with the recommendation. Otherwise decide and record why in 3 lines.
