# Notice

frugal is original work, but several ideas are adapted from **pstack** by Lauren Tan (poteto):
the playbook-per-task routing, and the principles "Attack the premise", "Subtract before you add",
"Build the lever", "Prove it works", "Model the domain", "Explain the number" and
"Sequence verifiable units". frugal rewrites them in its own words, in compressed form.
