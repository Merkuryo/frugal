# Write: documents, articles, emails, reports

1. **Reader and job.** Who reads it, what they must know or do after reading. One line each.
2. **Gather material first.** Facts, numbers and sources before prose. Use `research.md` or `analyze.md` if material is missing.
3. **Outline.** Headings or bullet skeleton. For anything over a page, show the outline before drafting if the human is present.
4. **Draft section by section** with `worker` or yourself. Lead each section with its point.
5. **Cut.** Remove filler, hedges, repetition and anything the reader does not need for the job. Prefer plain words and concrete examples.
6. **Check.** Every fact traced to a source, every number explained, tone matches the reader. Read it once as the reader.
7. **Deliver** in the format the human asked for; if none, the most editable one available.
